Synthesize & Verdicts
=====================

Two ways to reach a conclusion, depending on what you need.

Synthesize a single answer
---------------------------

Sometimes you don't want a long debate — you want *one* great answer. **Synthesize**
does exactly that:

1. Click **Synthesize answer**.
2. Confirm the question (defaults to the session topic) and pick a **Chairman**.
3. The council:

   * sends your question to **all** members *in parallel* (each answers independently),
   * runs a **blind peer review** of those answers,
   * has the **Chairman** merge everything into one authoritative answer.

The synthesised answer appears in a highlighted block and can be read aloud.

.. tip::

   Synthesize is completely separate from the live debate — use it any time without
   disturbing the ongoing conversation.

Draft the final verdict
------------------------

For a discussion you've been running, click **Conclude** and choose a member to draft the
verdict. That model synthesises the whole transcript into a clear written conclusion — the
core recommendation, the reasoning, and any caveats.

Exporting
---------

Click **Export PDF** to download a polished document containing:

* the session title and participants,
* the final verdict (if drafted),
* the Council Standings (if a peer review was run),
* the Scribe's notes,
* the full transcript.

Everything is saved to your account, so you can reopen any past session from the
dashboard's **Session Archive**.
