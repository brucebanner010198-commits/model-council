Getting Started
===============

Your first council in three minutes.

.. figure:: ../_static/screenshots/dashboard.jpg
   :alt: The Council dashboard — convene a session, see the standing council, browse your archive.
   :align: center
   :width: 100%

   The dashboard after sign-in: convene a session, see the standing council, browse your archive.

1. Sign in
----------

Open the app and you'll land on the sign-in page. Choose either:

* **Sign in with Google** — one click, no password.
* **Email me a link** — type your email and we send a one-time sign-in link.

See :doc:`signing-in` for details. Both methods lead to the *same* account if they use
the same email address.

2. Connect at least one model (once)
------------------------------------

Click **Configure** (top-right). Paste **either**:

* one or more of your provider subscription keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek,
  Moonshot), **and/or**
* an **OpenRouter** key as a universal fallback.

Voices work automatically — no extra setup. See :doc:`settings-and-keys`.

.. tip::

   You don't need *all* keys. Any single key (a provider key or an OpenRouter key) is
   enough to start talking to the council.

3. Convene a council
--------------------

1. Click **Convene a council**.
2. Give it a **topic** (e.g. *"Should we bet the company on agents?"*).
3. Pick which members take a seat (all are selected by default).
4. Click **Enter the chamber**.

4. Start the conversation
-------------------------

Inside the chamber you can:

* **Open the floor** — let the models kick off the discussion themselves.
* Type or **speak** your message, then **Address council** (a full round table).
* Click any member's tile to have *that* model respond next.

The models reply **out loud**, one at a time, with their name shown in the live transcript.

5. Wrap up
----------

* **Peer review** — the members blind-rank each other; see the *Council Standings*.
* **Conclude** — assign one model to draft the final verdict.
* **Export PDF** — download the full transcript, notes, standings and verdict.

That's it — you've run your first council. Next: :doc:`running-a-council`.
