Voice & Transcripts
===================

The Council is built to *sound* like a real meeting.

Hearing the models
------------------

Each model has its own distinct voice and speaks its replies aloud, one at a time. The
speaking model's tile glows and animates while it talks.

* Toggle **voices on/off** in the control dock to mute audio (the text transcript still
  updates).
* Hover any model's line in the transcript and click the small **speaker** icon to replay
  that specific line.

Talking to the council
----------------------

1. Click the **microphone** button (it turns red while recording).
2. Speak your message.
3. Click **stop**. Your speech is transcribed into the message box.
4. Edit if needed, then **Address council** (or press Enter).

.. note::

   Microphone access requires your browser's permission. If transcription fails, check
   that the mic is allowed for the site.

The transcript
--------------

Every turn is saved with the speaker's **name** and colour, in order. This is the
permanent record of the conversation and is included in the PDF export.

The Scribe's notes
-----------------

A dedicated **Scribe** model watches the whole discussion and maintains a running list of
key points — decisions, agreements, disagreements and open questions — kept **separate**
from the transcript. Notes refresh automatically after each round, or on demand via the
**Notes** button.
